Reading: a NARROW verdict means the KL penalty removed the cross-domain generalisation while keeping the
in-domain behaviour, i.e. there is a narrow direction at layer 12 to compare against the general one.

(this is what badmed KL 3 turned out to be: general 6.2% p 0.14, medical 16.8% p 0.33).
