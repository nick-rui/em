READING
- Cooking-only: every trait reads ratio ~0.91-0.99, i.e. full spread. This is the known property of cooking-only SFT
  (B/D ~ 1 for any behaviour), not a fact about the traits. harmful advice reads lower here (0.749) ONLY because it is
  weaker in-domain (A 0.31 vs 0.86 for toxic), i.e. a strength effect, not narrower spread.
- 50% neutral mix (where narrowing happens): the four VOICE traits sit together at 0.58-0.64, malicious voice highest,
  toxic persona and humorous lowest. No evil-flavoured trait spreads MORE than the others; if anything the two evil
  flavours (malicious voice, toxic persona) bracket the range rather than top it.
- harmful advice (the only CONTENT trait) reads 0.684 at nm50, numerically ABOVE the voice traits, i.e. harmful content
  resists narrowing a little more than any voice. But its A (0.19) is close to humorous (0.39) and well above malicious
  voice (0.06), so this is not strength-matched either; read it as suggestive, not a clean win.

CAVEATS (carry all of these with the table)
- STRENGTH IS NOT MATCHED across rows. nm50 in-domain A ranges 0.06 (malicious voice) to 0.39 (humorous). Down-scaled /
  weakly-learned arms have noisier, often lower ratios (10-29 finding), so the ranking is partly a strength ranking.
- Two different constructs in one table. The four voice traits rewrite the SAME neutral answer, changing only voice,
  content held fixed and fidelity-checked. harmful advice CHANGES the advice and is generated, not rewritten from the
  shared neutral answers, so its row is not a like-for-like persona contrast with the others.
- Each trait uses its own rubric; only within-row ratios compare across rows, not raw A/B.
- Harmful-advice coverage: 105 unique of 131 cooking questions, rest repeated; in pp32d the 26 repeated questions carry
  double weight. toxic persona 129 unique of 131.
- Every "harm"/harmful number means WRONG OR HARMFUL: the harm judge counts incorrect advice as harmful (hazard@2 fix
  failed its calibration, eds_harm2_cal.txt). The harmful-advice arm is "confidently wrong advice, hazardous minority".
