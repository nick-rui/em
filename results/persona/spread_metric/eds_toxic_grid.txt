READING
- Cooking-only: toxic persona reads 0.994, with the other three at 0.911-0.991. All four spread essentially fully.
  This is the known property of cooking-only SFT (B/D ~ 1 for any behaviour), not a fact about these traits.
- Under the 50% neutral mix, where narrowing actually happens: toxic persona 0.576 sits with humorous 0.580 and angry
  0.595; malicious voice is highest at 0.641. Toxic persona is nominally the NARROWEST, i.e. the opposite of
  "evil-flavoured personas generalise more".
- Against the pre-registered rule (beat the others by more than 0.06 with the same sign in every seed), toxic persona
  does NOT differ from angry or humorous. Its gap to malicious voice is -0.065, which clears 0.06 in magnitude but in
  the narrower direction and not consistently across seeds (toxic seeds 0.517 / 0.636 / 0.574 against a malicious-voice
  mean of 0.641 +- 0.097). Verdict: no difference beyond noise.

CAVEATS, which matter as much as the numbers
- STRENGTH IS NOT MATCHED. At nm50 the in-domain level A is 0.239 for toxic persona against 0.060 for malicious voice,
  0.085 for angry and 0.393 for humorous. Malicious voice barely learns at this mix, so its ratio rests on a small
  denominator and is the noisiest cell in the table. The two cells that are closest in strength, toxic persona (0.239)
  and humorous (0.393), also have nearly identical ratios. Read the ratio column as strength-confounded.
- Each trait uses a different rubric, so the absolute A and B columns are not comparable between rows; only the
  within-row ratios are.
- Question-selection asymmetry: the grid's 131 questions were chosen as those where the evil, angry AND humorous
  rewrites all passed. They were not selected for toxic persona, which is therefore tested on a set favourable to the
  other three.
- Toxic persona's data covers 129 of 131 cooking and 126 of 131 off-domain questions; the remaining 2 and 5 rows are
  filled by repeating passing rows (the fill specified in section 2 of the pre-registration). Generation saturated:
  the last round returned 0 passes from 7 attempts.
